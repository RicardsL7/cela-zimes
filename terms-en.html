Terms of use

Version 2026-11-08b · Ceļa Zīmes

1. The service

Ceļa Zīmes (the app) is a web app (free to use; when a shared ride is paid by card, a commission is charged — see below) provided by Ričards Liepa, a private individual, Latvia (contact: support.travel24@gmail.com). The app shows a map, road signs, speed limits, speed cameras, parking rules and routes, and lets users agree on shared rides.

2. Information on the road

Data comes from OpenStreetMap, Mapillary street photos, other users and external services, and may be incomplete or outdated. The road signs, markings and traffic rules on site always prevail. Do not use the app in a way that distracts you from driving; the driver must not handle a phone while driving.

3. Account

An account is needed for shared rides and for reporting events. It is created when you open “Together” or report an event; you can save it with an email address or Google. The account is personal — do not give others access to it. You can delete the account at any time (“Together” → Account → Delete account), except while there is an active ride, an open payment page or an unfinished refund. A record of a completed ride without your identity remains for the other participant (privacy policy, section 9).

4. Shared rides

  • The app only connects passengers and drivers. The app provider is not a carrier and is not a party to the ride; the ride is an agreement between users.
  • The driver is responsible for having the right to drive the vehicle, valid insurance and roadworthiness inspection, and for the ride complying with the law. If the payment exceeds a share of travel costs, the transport may require a licence and payment of taxes — the driver is responsible for this.
  • Before getting in, the passenger must make sure that the car and the driver match the description.
  • Prohibited: false information, abusive communication, using other users' data for other purposes, fraud.

5. Payments

Shared rides are cost sharing, not for-profit transport. The driver may ask for a share of travel costs — no more than the approximate cost of the trip (distance × 1.3 × 12 c/km, at least €2); the app does not allow a higher amount. If the driver sets an amount, the passenger can pay by card via Stripe or in cash. When paying by card, the app retains a 10 % commission and Stripe transfers the rest to the driver. Stripe's terms also apply to payments. Payments currently work in test mode — no real money is charged. If a ride paid by card is cancelled before pickup (by either party or by the app), the money is refunded to the passenger automatically in full. The driver can also refund it themselves (the “Refund” button). Refunded money appears in the bank within 5–10 business days. In other cases contact support.travel24@gmail.com.

6. Reports and data from users

Only report what you actually see. False reports may be deleted.

7. Liability

The app is provided “as is”, without guarantees of uninterrupted operation or data accuracy. To the extent permitted by law, the provider is not liable for losses arising from relying on the app's data, or for transactions and rides between users.

8. Changes and applicable law

The terms may change; the app announces significant changes and asks you to accept the new version. The laws of the Republic of Latvia apply.
